For agentic use of this repo the following files/folders are relevant:

* ./README.md is aimed at *human* users of the binary.
* ./agent-summary.md is aimed at *agents* wanting to *use* the binary.
  It contains clarifications that will be obvious to most humans
* ./.ai is aimed at *agents* working on the binary.
  Read ./.ai/AGENTS.md for further instructions to understand and change the code
  base.
